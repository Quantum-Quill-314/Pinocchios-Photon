# Security Policy

## Data Privacy & Image Handling
To protect user privacy and maintain ethical optical forensics, **we do not store, log, or cache any images uploaded by users.** 

When an image is submitted to the Omnikon platform for inspection, it is processed entirely in memory. Once the diagnostic heatmaps and confidence scores are rendered, the data is immediately vaporized from the system. 

**No user-uploaded media is ever saved or added to our training datasets.**

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability within this project's architecture, please open an issue in the repository. We take the integrity of our optical forge seriously
